===============================================================================
  CASTLE DANGER MUSIC COMPANY  //  MIX SYSTEM INSTALLER
===============================================================================

One installer for the whole Mix System: The Master, The Servant, The Freak,
Chop Shop, Dominatrix, BuzzKill, Flatliner and Low Blow. Tick the ones you
want (all are ticked to start with). No Terminal commands needed.


macOS  (CDMC_master_system_installer-macOS.pkg)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The installer isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time:

  1. Double-click the .pkg. macOS says it "can't be opened". Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message
     about "CDMC_master_system_installer-macOS.pkg" and click Open Anyway.
     Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  3. The installer opens. Click Continue, agree to the license, and on
     Installation Type untick anything you don't want. Install.

It puts everything where your DAW looks, for every user on this Mac:
  VST3        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  AU          /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  Apps        /Applications/Castle Danger Music Company   (standalone apps)
  Guides      /Applications/Castle Danger Music Company/Guides

It also:
  - clears the "downloaded from the internet" flag on what it installs, so
    you never need the xattr Terminal command;
  - moves any older copies you installed by hand into your own plug-in
    folders (~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins) to
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins Disabled/Castle Danger (older copies)
    so your DAW doesn't see two versions (delete that folder when you like);
  - resets the Audio Unit cache.

Then open Fender Studio Pro (or your DAW) and rescan plug-ins.

To update: run a newer installer over the top. To remove: delete the plugins
from the folders above.


Windows  (CDMC_master_system_installer-Windows.exe)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click the .exe. If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC,
     click More info, then Run anyway (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
  2. Choose the plugins, then Install.

  VST3        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  Apps        C:\Program Files\Castle Danger Music Company
  Guides      C:\Program Files\Castle Danger Music Company\Guides
  Start menu  Castle Danger Music Company

Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Uninstall from Settings > Apps.


Getting started
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Read "Mix System User Guide.pdf" in the Guides folder: it shows where each
plugin goes in your session and how The Master runs the rest.

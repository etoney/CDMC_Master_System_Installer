Castle Danger Music Company: Mix System

This installs the Mix System plugins: The Master, The Servant, The Freak, Cutthroat, Dominatrix, Dead Air, Flatliner and Low Blow. Each comes as VST3, Audio Unit and a standalone app, universal for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or later).

On Installation Type you can untick the ones you don't want.

Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins (VST3 and Components), the apps and guides to /Applications/Castle Danger Music Company. No Terminal commands are needed: the installer clears the download flag itself and resets the Audio Unit cache.

If you installed older copies by hand in your own plug-in folders, they are moved aside to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins Disabled/Castle Danger (older copies) so your DAW doesn't see two versions.
